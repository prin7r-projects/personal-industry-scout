# 07 · Sales Strategy — Personal Industry Scout

## Motion

**Self-serve subscription with a hand-bound onboarding.** PLG on the surface (the landing sells you in five minutes; the NOWPayments invoice is one click), human on the inside (intake call, named scout, watchlist signed by hand). We are not a sales-led product — there is no SDR/AE pipeline, no demo, no quote.

### Why these prices

- **$95**: just below the threshold where an Operator audience tries to expense it without explaining. Anchor against "two newsletters."
- **$245**: anchored to the Pitchbook seat ($20–40k/year ÷ 4 quarters ÷ industries the seat covers ≈ this number per industry).
- **$695**: anchored to "1 hour of consulting." The Concierge tier is positioned as 8h/month of a research analyst at $80–100/hr, so the math reads as 1/2-FTE-replacement-grade.

## Closing flow

1. **Land + skim**: visitor reads the hero memo; conversion-to-pricing scroll = ~70%.
2. **Pricing scan**: the three tiers read like a menu, not a wall. Most pick the middle.
3. **Click**: the **Subscribe · Partner** button on the middle card opens the NOWPayments invoice in-place. No account creation, no card form, no demo gate.
4. **Pay**: subscriber pays USDT/USDC.
5. **Activate**: IPN verifies, desk activates, scout sends Calendly within 10 min.
6. **Onboarding**: 30-minute call, watchlist signed, first Monday brief queued.

## Retention strategy

The brief itself is the retention loop. Subscribers stay if (and only if) the Monday brief is the first thing they read. We track:
- **Read-rate**: opens / sends, target ≥85%.
- **Reply-rate**: ad-hoc questions / brief, target ≥0.4 per subscriber per month — a reply means engagement.
- **Forward-rate**: forwards / brief (proxy via referral signups), target ≥4% — high-engagement subscribers forward.
- **Cancel cohort**: cancellations clustered by week / industry. We act on any industry with >7% monthly churn by talking to the analyst on that desk.

## What we will not do

- No discounts, no "first month free," no "limited-time" pricing. Quiet menu, fair price.
- No annual lock-in or claw-backs.
- No "customer success" playbook with health-score nags.
- No paid feature gating beyond what's listed in the tier table.
