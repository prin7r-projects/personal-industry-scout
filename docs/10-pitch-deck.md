## Slide 1 — Title

**Personal Industry Scout**
A research analyst on your desk, every Monday.

VOL · 14 · ISSUE · 19 · 2026

---

## Slide 2 — The problem

Senior operators spend 30–90 minutes every Sunday night assembling a brief on their industry.
- Pitchbook is a database, not a briefing.
- Newsletters write the average reader, not your watchlist.
- LLMs hallucinate names and dates.
- The associate they pay to do the work leaves in March.

---

## Slide 3 — The solution

A weekly five-minute briefing on the industry the subscriber actually runs.
- Written *for them*, against the watchlist they signed at intake.
- Distilled by a named human.
- Watermarked to the subscriber ID.
- Cancel any week.

---

## Slide 4 — Why now

- Stablecoin payment rails are now mainstream for high-ticket research subscribers.
- LLM-augmented research tools have collapsed the cost of producing a quality brief by ~80%.
- The senior-operator audience has *explicitly* tried "AI research" and explicitly does not want a product that leads with it.
- A named-human, accountable, signed brand is, suddenly, scarce.

---

## Slide 5 — Product

The hero of the landing page IS the product:
- 5-min memo, four signal items, signed by the lead scout, source-IDs on every claim.
- Three tiers — Operator $95, Partner $245, Concierge $695.
- Hosted invoice in USDT/USDC, paid monthly. Cancel any week.

---

## Slide 6 — Audience

Mid-cap and below. Senior operators. Family offices. Corp-dev leads. Principals.
- Hard qualifier: ≥$2k/year on existing research. 1–4 industries by name.
- Soft qualifier: pays an associate to "summarise" today.
- Total addressable: ~80–120k buyers in NA/EU/APAC.

---

## Slide 7 — Channels

- Peer-forwarded brief (the brief itself is the marketing).
- Hand-DM on LinkedIn from named scouts.
- Sample-brief PDFs as lead magnet.
- 4 newsletter sponsorships per year.
- 6 closed-door breakfasts per year.

No paid Google. No paid Meta. No SEO content. No SDR.

---

## Slide 8 — Unit economics

- ACV at mix: $245 × 12 = $2,940 (assume 60/30/10 split Operator/Partner/Concierge).
- Per-desk capacity: ~20 subscribers / lead scout / month.
- Lead-scout cost (loaded): $150–200k / yr.
- Break-even per desk: ~70 subscribers (Partner-mix).
- Gross margin at 100 subs/desk: ~75%.
