02 · THE PROBLEM

Sunday night, again.

Senior operators spend 30–90 minutes assembling a brief on their industry every Sunday night. They pay for tools that don't write briefs.

  • Pitchbook is a database, not a briefing.
  • Newsletters write the average reader, not your watchlist.
  • LLMs hallucinate names and dates without a human in the loop.
  • The associate they pay to do the work leaves in March.

A signed memo. Every Monday at 06:00.
