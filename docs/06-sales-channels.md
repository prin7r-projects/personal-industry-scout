# 06 · Sales Channels — Personal Industry Scout

The audience is small, expensive to reach via paid, and trust-led. We pick channels accordingly.

## Channel mix (year 1)

| Channel | Priority | Why it fits the audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder-led peer referral | A | The audience hires people who hire people; one DM from a peer beats $10k of cold outreach. Every brief is forwarded through a small private-DM funnel. |
| Targeted LinkedIn DMs (manual) | A | Marisa lives on LinkedIn. We hand-write 25 DMs/week to titled buyers who liked / commented on a known-good piece (e.g. a former IB analyst's post). Personal, never automated. |
| Sample-brief lead magnet | A | Hand-baked sample briefs for one industry, posted as a "this is what we send" PDF. Gated only by email — we don't want a heavy gate. |
| Paid newsletter sponsorships | B | One-off sponsorships of the *right* newsletters (e.g. The Generalist, Stratechery, Lenny's, Not Boring for the operator audience; Last Week in Health for vertical-health). We sponsor the brief itself, not an ad slot. |
| Conferences (closed-door) | B | Saastr, TechCrunch Disrupt corp-dev track, ACG conferences, Asia PE / FO summits. We do not exhibit; we run a 5-person private breakfast with one named scout. |
| Cold email | C | Used only for "in-bound replied-to" follow-ups; never spray-and-pray. The brief itself is the outbound — peer-forwarded — channel. |
| Twitter / X | C | Some use, but we do not invest. The audience reads more than they tweet. |
| Reddit / Discord | D | Not a fit. The audience does not buy from r/whatever. |
| Paid Google / Meta | D | Not viable at our price points; too expensive for this title set. |

## Channel-specific tactics

### Peer referral (A)
- Every paid subscriber gets a personalised, short referral note: "if you forward Marisa's brief to two peers and one signs up, you get a free month."
- The brief is **watermarked**, so a forward is traceable. We don't punish forwarding — we ask for credit.
- Our default *first* growth lever for the first 100 subscribers.

### LinkedIn manual DM (A)
- We watch 25 high-leverage posts/week (corp-dev, family-office, mid-cap PE, GM of vertical-SaaS) and reply with a 2-line note + the sample brief PDF link. Volume = ~100 DMs/month.
- Conversion target: 6–10% reply, ~1.5% paid conversion. ~1.5 paid subscribers/month from this channel by month 3.

### Sample-brief lead magnet (A)
- Twelve hand-baked sample briefs (one per industry vertical) live behind a single email gate. Each links to the Pricing section.
- The sample brief is the conversion vehicle — most subscribers convert *during* the read.

### Paid newsletter sponsorship (B)
- We sponsor *one brief* in The Generalist or Lenny's, written *as a brief*, signed *by a Personal Industry Scout analyst*. This shows the product, doesn't pitch it.
- Budget: $5–8k per placement; we run 4 in year 1.

### Conferences — private breakfasts (B)
- 5 people, named scout at the head of the table, one industry, one quiet venue. Walk-out conversion target: 60% of attendees subscribe within 30 days.
- Budget: $2–4k per breakfast (food + venue + scout travel).

## Out of scope in year 1

- TV / podcast ads.
- Affiliate programs that aren't trusted-peer.
- SEO content marketing — we are not a content well.
- Outbound SDR teams.

## North-star metric

Subscribers acquired *via forwarded brief from another subscriber* per month. If this number is climbing, the product is working. If it isn't, no paid channel saves us.
