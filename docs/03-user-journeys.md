# 03 · User Journeys — Personal Industry Scout

Three journeys the landing supports. The fourth (subscriber console) ships with `apps/app/`.

## Journey 1 — Discovery (Marisa, VP Corp Dev)

**Trigger**: a peer in her Slack DM forwards a screenshot of last week's Vertical SaaS · NA brief.

1. Marisa opens `personal-industry-scout.prin7r.com` on desktop.
2. The hero is a believable briefing memo (Vol 14 · Issue 19, dated 2026-05-04, signed J. Marsh). It mentions ServiceTitan, Squire, Toast — names she knows.
3. She skims the four briefing items in 90 seconds.
4. She scrolls to "How your scout works" — sees the three steps (Intake / Weekly / Escalate). Reads the "thirty-minute call, watchlist signed at the bottom by you" line.
5. She scans the Coverage grid. Vertical Software is the first column. She nods.
6. She lands on Pricing. Reads the **Partner** tier ($245/mo). Two industries, Thursday alerts, eight ad-hoc questions, 30-minute onboarding. That's exactly her week.
7. She clicks **Subscribe · Partner**. The CTA changes to "Opening invoice…". A NOWPayments hosted invoice opens in a new tab.

**Time to first value (TTFV)**: ~2 minutes — the moment the briefing memo hits her eyes.

## Journey 2 — First value (Marisa, after subscribing)

**Trigger**: NOWPayments confirms the USDT payment; IPN posts to the webhook; desk operator activates her subscription.

1. **+10 min**: she gets a one-line "welcome" email from her named scout (J. Marsh). It contains a Calendly for the 30-minute intake call.
2. **+24 h**: 30-minute intake call. They lock her watchlist — three competitor companies, eleven people, two regulators, two deal-size thresholds.
3. **Following Monday, 06:00 ET**: brief #1 lands. She reads it on her phone, on the train. Forwards one item to her CEO. Adds one person from the brief to her LinkedIn message-back queue.
4. **Thursday 18:00 ET**: alert digest lands. 90-second read. Three signal items.

**First "I'd cancel three things for this" moment**: brief #1 mentions a competitor pricing change she hadn't seen. Worth ~5 hours of associate time saved in week one.

## Journey 3 — Recurring use (Wei, single-family office Concierge)

**Trigger**: subscribed three weeks ago. The watchlist includes private credit, AI infra, vertical-SaaS, and climate hardware.

1. **Monday 06:00 SGT**: four briefs (one per industry) land in his Telegram thread.
2. **Wednesday 11:00 SGT**: a CFPB §1033 letter drops. His scout files a flash note within the hour.
3. **Wednesday 11:30 SGT**: Wei replies "thoughts on Squire down-round, allocator angle?" — gets a one-page reply within the day.
4. **Friday 14:00 SGT**: weekly 30-minute call with the named scout. Wei brings two questions and one named person he wants research on.
5. **Saturday**: he prints the four briefs and reads them at his desk on Sunday morning. He never opens a separate dashboard.

**Why he stays**: the named scout becomes a ~0.2 FTE he never has to hire. He'd cancel a $30k/year Pitchbook seat to keep this.
